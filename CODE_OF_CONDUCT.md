# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

LightClaw contributors, maintainers, and community members commit to a respectful,
harassment-free environment. We welcome different experience levels, identities,
backgrounds, and viewpoints.

Helpful participation includes:

- focusing criticism on behavior, evidence, and trade-offs rather than people;
- respecting privacy and never reposting another person's prompt, receipt, repository,
  logs, or identity without permission;
- accepting scoped maintainer decisions and the project's explicit non-goals;
- correcting mistakes openly and helping newcomers reproduce results.

Unacceptable participation includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language,
threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, publishing private data, or retaliating against a
good-faith reporter.

## Enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainer through the private address on
the maintainer's GitHub profile. For vulnerabilities, use
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/OthmaneBlial/lightclaw/security/advisories/new)
instead. Do not put sensitive evidence in a public issue or Discussion.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports are reviewed as privately and
promptly as practical. Conflicts of interest should be disclosed so another available
moderator can review the case.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, Discussions, issue tracker, release channels,
and public spaces where someone is representing LightClaw. Enforcement decisions should
be proportionate, documented privately, and centered on community safety.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
